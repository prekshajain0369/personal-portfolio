So, who I am

Hi, I’m Preksha Jain. I grew up in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh, where my school years at St. Mary’s gave me an early love for asking questions and then talking about the answers with anyone who would listen. That curiosity carried me to IIT Bombay, from where I graduated and where I learned as much from late-night conversations as from the classrooms.

Outside of all that, I’m passionate about vlogging — though not in the usual sense. I rarely post anything publicly; for me the camera is a way of holding on to moments, places and people I don’t want to forget. It’s a personal archive more than a channel.

If there’s one thing that ties it all together, it’s that I genuinely love talking and sharing what I know. Whether it’s a half-formed idea or something I just figured out, I’m happiest when I get to explain it to someone and see it click.
